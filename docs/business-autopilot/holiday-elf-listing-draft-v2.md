## Description
Twenty-four days of elf magic, planned for you, with a story your kids will actually follow.

The Twenty-Four Sparks of Christmas is one connected adventure from arrival night to Christmas Eve. Your holiday elf is searching for 24 missing Sparks. Every morning the family finds one, adds it to the Christmas Star, and the elf leaves a note. Every night the parent card tells you exactly what to set up, what you need, and what to do when time disappears.

YOU WILL RECEIVE
- Parent Command Center (9 pages): quick start, 24-day calendar, one-trip supply list, prep bins, rescue rules
- The Twenty-Four Sparks story + 24 elf notes (27 pages)
- Daily Parent Setup Cards (26 pages)
- Child Missions & Activity Cards (27 pages)
- Low-Ink Essential Props (20 pages)
- BONUS: The Parents' After-Hours Deck (5 pages, 36 cards) for the grown-ups after bedtime

Total: 6 PDF files, 114 printable US Letter pages.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Read the short chapter and the elf's note.
2. Kids draw, talk, play, or do the optional mission.
3. Add today's numbered Spark to the Christmas Star.
4. Use the parent card for the full scene, or the two-minute rescue.

BUILT FOR REAL PARENTS
- 24 connected story days with a real Christmas Eve ending
- 24 elf notes, 24 kid missions, 24 nightly setup cards
- Setups of about 5–15 minutes, plus a credible 2-minute rescue every day
- One-trip supply list, four weekly prep bins, household-first supplies
- Printable star map, numbered Sparks, footprints, tickets, hats, lantern, reindeer, sleigh and more
- No behavior grading, no reporting, no naughty-list pressure
- Bonus parent deck: 12 elf excuses, 12 parent dares, 12 confessions. PG. For adults only, after the kids are asleep.

IMPORTANT
- Digital download. Nothing is shipped.
- A physical elf doll, food, decorations, gifts, and craft supplies are not included.
- Print at home or at a local print shop. Full-color pages; a low-ink props file is included.
- Adult supervision required for scissors, small pieces, food, and battery lights.
- Personalization is not included in this version.
- Personal household use only. No resale, redistribution, or commercial use.
- Original holiday elf story and character. Not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any branded elf company.
