## 1. Niche validation

You're not picking a niche from scratch — you already have one with a built product. The question is whether it's worth the growth effort. It is, with one caveat:

- **Demand driver:** Pregnancy is a fixed 40-week window with a built-in reason to buy *now* (it expires). That's a rare forcing function most niches don't have — no "someday" purchases.
- **Format fit:** Carousels and Reels both work here. Carousels sell the vault directly (show real pages/spreads — Etsy's own rule that Image 1 must show real product applies to IG too: don't lead with a stylized mockup). Reels work for reach (pose tutorials, transformation, trending audio).
- **Market signal:** Maternity/bump content has a large audience but low creator-to-demand friction — it's saturated with *photographers* selling sessions, but not with *creators selling a $12 idea system*. That's your wedge: you're not competing with photographers, you're solving "I don't know what to do when I book one."
- **Caveat:** the field guide itself flags the buyer persona as "not yet finalized." Don't skip that — the calendar below assumes "expecting moms who want to DIY their own bump photos," not moms who hire a photographer. If your actual buyer is closer to the latter, the CTA changes (from "do this yourself" to "bring this list to your photographer").

**Recommendation:** proceed. Don't spend more time validating — the product already sells on Etsy, which is the real validation. Spend the time on distribution instead.

## 4. Competitor pattern audit (do this in 30 minutes, don't skip it)

I have no ability to browse or scrape live Instagram accounts in this session, so I can't hand you an analysis of specific competitors' actual posts — anyone claiming to have "analyzed the top 10 viral posts in your niche" without opening Instagram is fabricating it. Here's the real method, and it takes less time than reading a fake report would:

**Method:**
1. Search `#pregnancyphotoideas`, `#maternityphotography`, `#bumpphotos`, `#pregnancyannouncement` — sort by "Recent" and "Top."
2. Open the 10 highest-engagement posts (compare likes to the account's follower count, not raw likes — a 5K-follower account with 2K likes beats a 500K account with 3K likes).
3. For each, log: opening frame/hook text, format (carousel/reel/static), pacing, CTA, caption opener.
4. Look for what repeats across 3+ posts — that's the real pattern, not any single post.

**Patterns that reliably perform in this content category** (general content-marketing knowledge, not a claim about specific accounts):
- **Text-hook carousels** — first slide is bold text stating the value ("47 bump poses you haven't tried"), not a photo alone. The text *is* the hook.
- **Before/after or progression format** — "8 weeks vs. 38 weeks" style content has built-in narrative tension.
- **Numbered list format** — "5 poses," "3 mistakes" — these outperform open-ended captions because they promise a specific, finishable payoff.
- **Caption-as-value** — in this niche specifically, a strong caption people screenshot (a "letter to my bump" template, e.g.) gets saved even if the photo is average.
- **Relatable self-deprecation** — "things no one told me about trimester 3" format drives comments because it invites people to add their own.

Use this list as your hook-writing checklist, not a substitute for actually looking.

## 6. DM automation — Meta native (no third-party tool)

**Switched from ManyChat to Meta's built-in automation** — free, no new subscription, already tied to your account. Set up in **Meta Business Suite → Automation → Instagram → "Reply to comments with a keyword."**

**Trade-off, stated plainly:** native Meta automation does a single trigger → one message (optionally with a link button). It cannot do the timed multi-day drip or branch based on what someone replies — that's what a paid tool like ManyChat is actually for. At current comment volume, one well-written instant message captures most of the value anyway; the follow-up/nurture steps below are worth doing personally and manually once someone's engaged, not automating.

**Trigger:** comment containing the word **"BUMP"** on any post.

**Automated message (single step, sent instantly):**
> Hey! 👋 Here's the link to the full 100 Pregnancy Photo Ideas vault — $12, instant download, 11 collections (window light, outdoor, partner poses, artistic, and more): [link]
> No pressure, it's yours whenever you want it. 💛

Set the button label to "Get the Vault" or similar, linking straight to the Etsy listing (or a link-in-bio page if you want to track clicks separately).

**Manual follow-up (do this yourself, not automated):** if someone replies asking a question instead of clicking, answer personally — at this volume that's minutes of work per week, not worth building automation for yet. If the bundle goes live, mention it in your reply rather than building a second automated rule.

**Revisit ManyChat if/when:** comment volume gets high enough that manual follow-up becomes real time (a genuine "too many to answer by hand" problem, not a hypothetical one) — that's the point where the subscription pays for itself.

## 8. Weekly operating SOP (target: 30 min/day, batched)

**Sunday (60–90 min) — Plan & batch:**
- Pull next week's 4–5 posts from the calendar in Section 3
- Write all captions in one sitting (use the hook + why-it-works column as a guide)
- Pull/select the real vault-page images needed for that week's carousels

**Monday (60 min) — Design:**
- Build all carousels/Reels in Canva using a saved brand template
- Export and drop into scheduler

**Tuesday (20 min) — Schedule:**
- Load the week into Meta Business Suite (free, covers Instagram + Facebook — no separate scheduler subscription needed)
- Confirm the Meta automation rule (Section 6) is still active and its trigger keyword matches what's in this week's captions

**Daily (15 min):**
- Reply to comments and DMs not caught by automation
- Note anything that's over/under-performing vs. your average

**Friday (30 min) — Review:**
- Check Insights: saves, reach, profile visits, link clicks
- Flag the week's best-performing post/hook for the "advance phase" criteria in Section 7

**Tools:** Canva (design) · Meta Business Suite, free (scheduling + basic analytics + DM automation) · a simple spreadsheet or the table in Section 3 (content calendar) · Etsy/Shopify dashboards (sales tracking). No paid subscriptions required to run this at current volume.

**If you hand this to a VA, the 80% they can own:** captioning from the calendar template, Canva design from a saved template, scheduling, comment replies using 3-4 saved responses, weekly Insights screenshot. **What stays with you:** approving captions before they post, responding to DMs that go beyond the automation script, and the Friday performance review.

**QC checklist before anything goes live:**
- [ ] Caption matches brand voice (warm, direct, not salesy)
- [ ] CTA present (link in bio, "comment BUMP," or explicit price)
- [ ] Link in bio is correct and current
- [ ] First image is real product, not a stylized mockup (same rule as the Etsy listings)
- [ ] Hashtags relevant, not copy-pasted from an unrelated post
