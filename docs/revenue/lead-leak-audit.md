## The offer

>

**What they get:**

1. **Your current customer journey, mapped** — every public way a customer can reach you (website form, Instagram DM, phone, text, booking link) and what each one actually asks for.
2. **What's missing before you can quote** — the service-specific details your estimator needs that no current intake path collects.

**What it is not:** a proposal, a sales call, or a software demo. A written document. No meeting required unless they want one.

Your launch kit says *don't display exact pricing publicly during validation*. That rule protects the **implementation** price, which is genuinely scope-dependent — keep it. An audit is the opposite case: fixed scope, small number, decided in one message. Naming $297 is what turns it from "let's talk" into "sure, send it." Treat this as the documented exception.

## Rules for delivering these

Carried straight from your launch kit, and they matter more now that money is attached:

- **Never submit a fake quote request or impersonate a customer.** Not to time a response, not to test the form. If you want real response-time data, ask permission and identify the test.
- **No invented statistics.** Don't write "you're losing 30% of leads." You don't know that. Write what you observed and why it plausibly costs jobs.
- **Distinguish observation from hypothesis.** "Your form asks for name and email only" is an observation. "This likely means your estimator is going back and forth before quoting" is a hypothesis — label it as one.
- **Respect what already works.** Several of these shops have genuinely strong intake — ProFilm's price-by-text, Wrap Me USA's same-day WhatsApp quote. Praising those honestly and finding the leak elsewhere is far more credible than manufacturing a problem.
- **If you find nothing significant, say so and refund.** A shop with a tight journey is a shop that will remember you were honest. That's worth more than $297.

## Fulfillment checklist

Per audit, ~90 minutes:

- [ ] Review the website quote path; record what it asks for
- [ ] Review Instagram bio, buttons, highlights, CTAs
- [ ] Note whether intake is service-specific or generic
- [ ] Check for visible deposit, scheduling, media collection
- [ ] Read a sample of recent public reviews for communication patterns
- [ ] Record the public next step: form, DM, call, text, booking
- [ ] Write 3 leaks with evidence URLs
- [ ] Rank them; pick the one recommendation
- [ ] Proofread against the rules above
- [ ] Send as PDF within 48 hours
