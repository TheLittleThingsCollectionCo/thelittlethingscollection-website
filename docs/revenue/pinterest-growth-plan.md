# Pinterest traffic plan — The Little Things Collection

**Built:** 2026-09-19. Companion to `docs/revenue/instagram-growth-plan.md`. This is the channel flagged there as the fastest real-dollar lever, built out.

**Context check:** `docs/twelveten-strategic-field-guide.md` already names Pinterest as a planned "evergreen acquisition" channel, gated on "after conversion is demonstrated" (line 321, line 1698). Bump Stories already sells on Etsy — that condition is met. This is that gate opening, not a new idea outside the plan.

**What I could not verify:** no existing Pinterest account for the brand turned up in the repo or field guide — this assumes you're starting from zero. If a business account already exists, skip Section 1 and audit what's there against Sections 2–4 instead of rebuilding it.

**Why this channel specifically, before others:** Pinterest is a visual search engine, not a social feed — a pin can drive traffic for years after posting, unlike an Instagram post that's dead in 48 hours. For a $12 evergreen digital download with no shipping and no restock, that compounding is worth more than most other channels at this stage.

---

## 1. Account setup (do this first, ~20 minutes)

1. Create a **Pinterest Business account** (free) — not a personal account. If one exists personally, convert it rather than starting a second one.
2. Claim both:
   - Your Etsy shop (Pinterest has a direct Etsy claim option)
   - Your website domain, if `site/` or a storefront domain is live
   Claiming shows your logo on pins linking there and unlocks analytics.
3. Enable **Rich Pins** (specifically Product Pins) — pulls price/availability automatically onto the pin so it reads as shoppable, not just an image.
4. Fill out the profile: business name "The Little Things Collection," bio stating what you make (photo idea vaults) and who it's for (expecting/new moms), link to your Etsy shop or link-in-bio page.

---

## 2. Board structure

Pinterest ranks pins partly by board relevance, so board names should be searched phrases, not cute names. Create these to start:

| Board name | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Pregnancy Photo Ideas | Primary board — pins directly promoting Bump Stories |
| Maternity Photoshoot Poses | Secondary keyword variant, catches different search terms |
| Newborn Photo Ideas | Ready for when that vault ships |
| Baby Announcement Ideas | Feeds the Announcement Moments mini-vault |
| Pregnancy Tips & Prep | Educational content, softer sell, builds account authority |
| Family Photo Ideas | Feeds Family Memories mini-vault |

Pin every new pin to its most relevant board **plus** one broader board if it fits (e.g., a bump-pose pin also goes to "Maternity Photoshoot Poses"). Don't pin the same image to more than 2–3 boards — Pinterest treats over-distribution as spam-like behavior.

---

## 3. Pin SEO (this is what actually drives traffic — don't skip it)

Pinterest is closer to Google than to Instagram: it matches text, not just engagement. Every pin needs:

- **Pin title** (100 char max): lead with the search phrase, not a clever hook. E.g. `100 Pregnancy Photo Ideas | Bump Poses, Props & Captions` — not `You NEED to see this!`
- **Pin description** (500 char max): 2-3 sentences using natural keyword variations (pregnancy photo ideas, bump photos, maternity poses, pregnancy announcement) plus a soft CTA. Write it like a mini product description, not a caption.
- **Board it's pinned to**: matches the topic (see Section 2).
- **Alt text**: fill it in — it's an additional ranking signal most people skip.

Reuse the keyword list already in `docs/revenue/etsy-listings.md` tags — those were researched for Etsy's own search algorithm and transfer directly (pregnancy photos, bump photos, maternity poses, photo prompts, pregnancy gift, expecting mom, etc).

---

## 4. Pin design specs

- **Size:** 1000 x 1500px (2:3 ratio) — Pinterest's preferred format, gets more real estate in feed.
- **Text overlay:** put the hook as bold text on the image itself (same principle as the IG carousel first-slide rule in Section 3 of the Instagram plan) — e.g. "100 Bump Photo Ideas" overlaid on a real vault preview page.
- **Branding:** small logo or "The Little Things Collection" watermark in a corner, low-opacity — enough for recognition, not so much it looks like an ad.
- **Source image:** same rule as Etsy Image 1 and IG's first grid slide — lead with the real product page, not a stylized mockup.
- **Video pins:** Pinterest now favors these in distribution. A 6-10 second looping clip flipping through 3-4 vault pages, with text overlay, works well and can be repurposed straight from an Instagram Reel export.

---

## 5. Content sourcing — repurpose, don't recreate

You already have a 30-day content calendar in `docs/revenue/instagram-growth-plan.md` Section 3. Every "Proof" and "Offer" pillar post there is a pin candidate:

- Each **carousel** → split into 2-4 individual static pins (Pinterest doesn't do true carousels the way Instagram does — pin the strongest 2-4 slides separately, each with its own title/description).
- Each **Reel** → export and re-upload as a Pinterest video pin.
- New pins can also be created directly from Etsy listing images (Pinterest has a "pin from Etsy" flow when the shop is claimed).

This means Pinterest adds close to zero new content-creation work if you're already running the Instagram calendar — it's a distribution layer on top, not a second content job.

---

## 6. Posting cadence

- **Minimum:** 3-5 new pins/week to start (Week 1-2), scaling to 1/day by Week 4 as the repurposing workflow gets fast.
- Consistency matters more than volume — Pinterest's algorithm favors accounts that post regularly over ones that post 20 pins one day and nothing for two weeks.
- No need to delete or "clean up" old pins — unlike Instagram, an old pin that's still driving traffic should stay up indefinitely.

---

## 7. Linking strategy

- Link every pin to the **Etsy listing directly** (simplest, keeps the whole funnel inside Etsy's checkout — no cart-abandonment risk from an extra site hop) unless you want to build an email list first, in which case link to a landing page with an opt-in instead.
- If you want to measure Pinterest's contribution separately from other channels, add a UTM parameter to the Etsy link (e.g. `?utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=bumpstories`) — Etsy's own stats won't separate traffic sources otherwise.

## 9. What to watch, and when to know it's working

Check Pinterest Analytics (built into the business account) weekly:
- **Impressions and saves** — early signal, shows if titles/descriptions are ranking
- **Outbound clicks** — the number that actually matters (traffic to Etsy)
- **Which board/pin is driving clicks** — double down on that keyword cluster

**Realistic timeline:** Pinterest is slower to start than Instagram (it can take 2-4 weeks for pins to gain search traction) but compounds — expect it to look unimpressive in Week 1-2 and then start contributing steady, low-effort traffic by Week 6-8 without additional posting volume. Don't judge it on the same weekly cadence as Instagram.
