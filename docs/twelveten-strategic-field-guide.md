## How to Use This Book

This is the collective map of the TwelveTen ecosystem. It separates customer-facing businesses from product lines, internal publishing infrastructure, marketing channels and future software so that every idea has a home without becoming an equal priority.

### The operating hierarchy

1. Choose one primary cash engine for a 30-day sprint.
2. Maintain one secondary authority-based offer.
3. Use existing products as small, bounded experiments.
4. Capture—not build—everything in later tiers.
5. Promote an idea only after demand, offer, buyer and revenue model are validated.

| Level | Meaning | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Human-owned | Requires judgment, consent, accountability or relationship. | Clinical conclusions, legal claims, pricing approval, sales calls, final publication. |

### 1. Marellstone & Co. — AI Front Office and Revenue Operations

> **ROLE** — Primary scalable cash engine

Marellstone installs and manages an AI-powered front office for local service businesses so they capture missed leads, schedule faster and follow up consistently.

#### Offer and monetization ladder

- Paid diagnostic or missed-lead audit.
- Implementation/setup fee for phone, SMS, CRM, routing and scheduling.
- Monthly management, optimization and reporting retainer.
- Add-ons: website chat, reactivation campaigns, review requests, quoting workflows, dashboards and advertising handoff.
- Later: niche-specific templates, white-label implementation, licensing and productized onboarding.

#### What AI can own

- Build prospect lists from approved public sources.
- Research each company's call flow, reviews, hours and likely leakage points.
- Draft personalized cold emails, DMs, call openers and follow-up sequences.
- Generate demo scripts, synthetic call scenarios, FAQs and objection handling.
- Draft workflow maps, SOPs, onboarding forms, proposals, reports and client training.
- Summarize call transcripts and produce weekly performance reports.
- Monitor pipeline fields and flag overdue follow-up.

#### What requires Danielle or another human

- Choose one niche, buyer and promise.
- Speak with owners and conduct discovery.
- Approve claims, pricing, contracts, data handling and implementation scope.
- Configure/test real phone, CRM and scheduling behavior.
- Handle exceptions, complaints and relationship management.
- Approve every client-facing deliverable and ensure consent/compliance.

#### Minimum monetization sequence

1. Choose wrap/tint/detail shops as the first bounded niche.
2. Build one functioning demonstration using a fictional or consented business.
3. Create one-page offer, pricing range and implementation boundaries.
4. Build a list of 25 qualified local prospects.
5. Conduct daily outreach and book discovery calls.
6. Close one paid pilot; document baseline, result and testimonial.
7. Productize only what repeated across the pilot.

#### Guardrails

- Do not promise revenue you cannot attribute.
- Do not deploy untested automations into a live business.
- Confirm call recording, messaging consent, privacy and industry-specific requirements.
- Human escalation must always exist.

## Part III — AI Delegation Operating System

### Recommended AI workstreams

| AI workstream | Primary responsibilities | Human checkpoint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chief of Staff | Daily priorities, project status, decision briefs, blockers. | Danielle selects priorities and authorizes changes. |
| Research Analyst | Source gathering, summaries, competitor/market briefs. | Human verifies source quality and conclusions. |
| Sales Development | Prospect research, personalization, outreach drafts, CRM hygiene. | Human approves target, sends sensitive outreach and conducts calls. |
| Offer Architect | Scope, pricing scenarios, packages, objection library. | Human sets promises, economics and boundaries. |
| Content Studio | Hooks, scripts, repurposing, calendars, briefs. | Human supplies real voice, footage, claims and final approval. |
| Product Editor | Outlines, instructions, consistency, indexing, version comparison. | Subject-matter and clinical review. |
| Operations Builder | SOPs, forms, workflows, dashboards and QA tests. | Human grants access, configures live systems and accepts results. |
| Analytics | Weekly funnel, conversion and revenue summaries. | Human interprets context and decides action. |
| Archive Librarian | Classify approved materials, detect duplicates and link sources. | Human authorizes restructuring/deletion. |

### Tasks AI should never independently own

- Clinical conclusions or standards-of-care opinions
- Legal advice, legal filings or custody strategy
- Medication or treatment recommendations
- Contracts, pricing commitments or refunds outside policy
- Sending emotionally sensitive messages without review
- Publishing private details about children, partners, patients or litigation
- Moving money, accepting clients or granting system access without authorization
- Final factual approval of any regulated product

### The delegation packet for every task

1. **Outcome:** what "done" means.
2. **Inputs:** approved sources, files and facts.
3. **Boundaries:** what may not be claimed, changed or sent.
4. **Format:** exact deliverable and audience.
5. **Quality check:** citations, calculations, formatting and completeness.
