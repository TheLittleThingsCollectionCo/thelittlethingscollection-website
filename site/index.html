The training was never the hard part. Proving it is.

Most agencies believe their ADRD files are fine. The citations AHCA has issued weren't for failing to train — they were for failing to prove it: a specific record, for a specific worker, with a specific date, produced while a surveyor waits.

What AHCA actually cited under CZ875

  1. No written ADRD information provided at the time of hire
  2. No proof of the 1-hour DOEA training completed within 30 days
  3. No proof of the 2-hour training completed within 7 months for personal care staff

Five nurse registries have been cited so far. Every one of these is a document — either it's in the personnel file, or it isn't.
