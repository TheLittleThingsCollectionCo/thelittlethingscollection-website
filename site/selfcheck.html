These are the three records AHCA cited a nurse registry for

Florida's Agency for Health Care Administration has issued a citation under core tag CZ875 for ADRD non-compliance. The three documented failures were:

  1. No written ADRD information provided at the time of hire
  2. No proof of the 1-hour training completed within 30 days
  3. No proof of the 2-hour training completed within 7 months for personal care staff

You just answered for all three.

What this is not

  • This is an estimate built from what you typed, not a review of your files. It is not a finding.
  • It is not legal advice, and it is not a prediction of what a surveyor would conclude.
  • It cannot tell you whether a record you do hold is actually adequate — only a file review does that.
  • Nothing here represents that any course or provider is state-approved.
  • If your files are already clean, this will overstate your exposure. That is the honest limit of a self-report.
